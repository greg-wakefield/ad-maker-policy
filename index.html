ad-maker — Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

ad-maker turns Figma designs into ad templates and renders them into ad images that you can publish to advertising accounts you already control. It is operated by Greg Wakefield ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we hold, why, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Connections you authorise. Access and refresh tokens for the accounts you connect — Figma, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads — plus the identifiers of the ad accounts, pages and campaigns you choose to work with.
  • Content you put in. Templates imported from Figma, spreadsheet feeds you upload, the ad images and videos we render from them, and the ad copy you write.
  • Operational records. Logs of renders and publishes, and the responses the advertising platforms return, so that failures can be diagnosed.

How we use it

Only to run the service you asked for: signing you in, reading the designs and feeds you point us at, rendering ads, and creating or updating ads in the advertising accounts you have connected. We act on your instruction — we never publish an ad you have not asked for.

Meta data in particular

We use Meta's Marketing API with the permissions you grant. That access is used to list the ad accounts and pages you administer, to upload creative, and to create and update ads in the accounts you select. We do not read your personal Facebook or Instagram profile content, your friends, or your messages, and we do not use Meta data for anything other than operating your account with us.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, build advertising profiles of you, or use your content to train machine-learning models.

Who else sees it

Service providers who host the application, its database and its file storage, acting on our instructions and bound to confidentiality. The advertising platforms themselves (Meta, Google) receive exactly the creative and campaign data needed to place the ads you request. We also disclose data where the law requires it.

Where it is kept and for how long

Data is stored on servers in the EU and the United States. We keep it while your account is active. When you disconnect a platform we delete the stored tokens for it. When you close your account we delete your workspace content, and back-ups age out within 30 days.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a platform in Settings deletes the tokens we hold for it. To erase everything, email the address below from your account address with the subject "Delete my data"; we confirm and delete your account, connections, uploads and rendered files within 30 days. You can also remove ad-maker under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, which revokes our access to your Meta data immediately.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below. If you are in the UK or the EEA you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Cookies

We set a single cookie to keep you signed in. We run no advertising or third-party tracking cookies on this site.

Children

ad-maker is a tool for advertisers and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and tell account holders by email.
